Pipeline Architecture & Implementation Highlights

1

Intelligent Normalization

User drawings undergo automatic aspect-ratio-preserving 20×20 bounding box scaling, padding to 28×28, and center-of-mass translation to perfectly replicate the MNIST data distribution.

2

Dual-Block Deep CNN

Stack of 3×3 convolutions (32 & 64 filters) with Batch Normalization, Max Pooling, and Dropout regularizers (0.25 & 0.40) to minimize overfitting and achieve 99.2%+ accuracy.

3

Comparative Benchmark

Side-by-side verification with an MLP (Multilayer Perceptron) reveals why convolutional kernels excel at detecting localized edge and curve features over dense flat vectors.
